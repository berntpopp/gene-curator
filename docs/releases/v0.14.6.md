# v0.14.6

## Added

- **No primary kidney association (kidney-genetics-db#535).** A reviewed outcome for genes
  with no primary kidney gene-disease association. The KGDB methodology bundle gains a third
  workflow pair, `KGDB No Kidney Association`:
  - Its precuration names the gene only (`relationship_type: no_association`). Its
    precuration stage sets `approve_on_submit`, so submit approves it and opens the draft
    curation (`curation_id` in the submit result).
  - The curation records `rationale`, `sources_checked[]`, `non_kidney_entities[]` (with
    `kidney_phenotype_via`) and `evidence_summary`. The judge reviews it once (4-eyes;
    `review_policy=ai` works). The verdict is `no_primary_kidney_association`.
- **Export:** `relationship_type` takes a new value, `no_association`, for these items
  (`entity_key` `""`, null disease, MONDO and MOI fields).
- **Guard:** an assessment covers the whole gene in its scope. An approved Mendelian or
  susceptibility curation of the gene archives it, and an assessment cannot become active
  while one is active (409 naming that curation).

## Deploy

Re-import `docs/schemas/kidney-genetics/kgdb-methodology.json` (platform admin) to create the
new pair. The existing KGDB schemas are unchanged.
